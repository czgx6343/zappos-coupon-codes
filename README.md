## Where Zappos codes actually come from

Zappos's own Coupons & Promotions page doesn't list a single code. It's a landing page pointing you toward three channels: the VIP program, the email newsletter, and periodic sale events. That surprises people who expect a public coupon wall.

The three channels, in order of usefulness:

1. **Email newsletter.** New subscribers get a promo code for 10% off (one use per previously-unsubscribed email address). It's the most universally applicable code Zappos hands out, since it lacks the category restrictions that come with most published codes.
2. **The Zappos app.** The app runs exclusive coupons that never appear on the website. It also sends push notifications when items you've viewed drop in price or come back in stock.
3. **Seasonal sale events.** Zappos runs a predictable calendar: a Two-Day Sale in the second week of October with a 20% code, Black Friday with up to 40% off sitewide plus a $50-and-under section, and Cyber Monday with an extra 25% off sale items. If your purchase can wait a few weeks, the Black Friday window has historically been the deepest discount event of the year.

If a code from a third-party site doesn't work, the usual culprits are brand or category exclusions in the fine print, an expired window, or a typo. Zappos's checkout won't always tell you which one it is — it just declines the code.
